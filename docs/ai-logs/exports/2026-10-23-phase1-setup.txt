  5. Reentrancy risk not captured in spec or invariants

  AssetSale.withdrawProceeds() will send ETH to the issuer. The spec says "sends
  full ETH balance to issuer" but doesn't specify the call pattern. If
  implemented with .call{value: ...}("") (the correct modern pattern), the
  issuer wallet could be a contract that re-enters withdrawProceeds before the
  balance is zeroed, draining more ETH than they are owed. This is a known,
  predictable vulnerability that the spec should either: (a) require a
  checks-effects-interactions pattern or a reentrancy guard, or (b) name as a
  known risk. Currently neither appears anywhere.

  6. deauthoriseIssuer behavior is underspecified

  addToWhitelist? The issuer address is stored directly on each contract —
  deauthorising them at the factory level has no effect on what they can do with
  their own token. This spec gap will either cause a confusing audit finding
  ("issuer can still act after deauthorisation") or a design argument in Phase
  5.
