# Subscription offering specification

Authority: [verbatim enhancement request](spec/subscription-requirements.md), 7 November 2026. This additive specification supersedes the historical exclusion of subscription issuance only. Existing direct ETH sales remain unchanged. No commits or public deployments are authorised by this implementation task.

## Decisions and function traceability

- Requirements 3–5: owner-minted MockUSD (six decimals), fixed zero-decimal AssetToken inventory, price in mUSD base units. Only the factory-pinned MockUSD is supported by the application.
- Requirements 4–16: a separate SubscriptionFactory references the existing AssetFactory and preserves its constructor, asset IDs, ABI and direct sales. Only a currently authorised issuer may create an offering for its own registered asset. Independent escrow balances prevent inventory double spending.
- Requirements 5–8: immutable parameters; start strictly after creation; end after start; hard cap is computed as supply times price; soft cap is positive and at most hard cap. Partial issuer deposits are allowed strictly before start; exact received amounts and full inventory are required before commitments. Commitments are positive price multiples, may be topped up, and may exceed hard cap.
- Requirements 9–11: anyone may finalise once at/after end. Under subscription, accepted funding equals commitments. Over subscription, guaranteed allocated units are `max(0, supply - subscriberCount + 1)`; success requires that lower bound times price to meet soft cap. Conservative false failures are possible and explicitly disclosed. Zero commitments and underfunded inventory fail. No investor enumeration occurs.
- Requirements 10–15: `settleSubscription` settles the caller; `settleFor` allows anyone to settle another subscriber's accounting without moving assets, improving liveness. Entitlements use full-precision `Math.mulDiv`. Settlement, refund, claim and issuer withdrawal are distinct guarded operations. Only the entitlement owner can withdraw or claim; no claim expiry exists. Eligibility is checked by AssetToken on delivery, never on refund/settlement.
- Requirements 14–15: track commitments, settled commitments, refund credits/paid refunds, recognised/withdrawn proceeds and allocated/claimed units. Outstanding payment liabilities are unsettled deposits + unpaid refunds + unwithdrawn proceeds. Donations are not proceeds. Before every subscriber is settled, reserve the maximum possible allocation less claims; afterwards reserve exact allocated units less claims. Failure reserves no token claims. Recovery never consumes this reserve.
- Requirements 17–18: integrate creation, funding, subscriptions, finalisation and settlement into the existing React app; amounts use BigInt and six-decimal formatting. Demand is distinct from guaranteed/recognised accepted funding. Existing transaction status handling is reused.
- Requirements 19–26: test baseline, boundaries, exact examples, rounding, order independence, conservation, malicious callbacks, donations, access control, actual service integration and a local reproducible demo. Record measured results and limitations, without inventing audit findings.

## Trust and liveness

The original AssetToken issuer/default administrators retain pause, revocation and forced-transfer powers. They can prevent delivery or seize a revoked offering's inventory; this inherited trust cannot be removed without changing AssetToken. Refunds remain independent of token eligibility. Payment tokens must be honest standard non-rebasing, non-fee MockUSD. Strict transfer deltas reject common incompatible behavior, but cannot make a malicious balanceOf truthful. Finalisation/settlement require transactions, not a server. Whitelisted wallet splitting can lower the conservative success bound; use suitable supply granularity and a soft-cap margin. No claim expiry or arbitrary confiscation is added.
