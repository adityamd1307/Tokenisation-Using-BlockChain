Tokenisation of Real-World and Digital Assets on Ethereum

The project would be inspired by the increasing adoption of asset tokenisation by major financial institutions such as BlackRock, J.P. Morgan, and DTCC. We aim to develop a simplified dApp demonstrating how traditionally managed assets can be represented, issued, transferred, and managed using blockchain-based tokens and smart contracts.

For our proposed MVP, we are considering the following features:

Asset tokenisation: An authorised issuer can create/tokenise an asset using an Ethereum token standard.
Investor wallet interaction: Users can connect their wallets and view their tokenised asset holdings.
Primary issuance and transfer: Users can acquire and transfer tokenised assets through smart contracts.
Ownership and transaction records: Asset ownership and transaction history are maintained transparently on-chain.
Issuer controls/compliance: Basic mechanisms such as whitelisting authorised investors or restricting transfers to approved addresses.
From a technical perspective, we plan to implement the system using Solidity smart contracts on Ethereum, together with a web-based frontend and wallet integration such as MetaMask. We would also explore relevant token standards and smart-contract mechanisms used for tokenised financial assets.

The broader problem we would like to explore is how blockchain can provide programmable ownership, transparent settlement, and more efficient transfer of assets, while also accounting for the permissioning and compliance requirements that distinguish institutional asset tokenisation from ordinary cryptocurrencies.
